Privacy

Privacy Policy

The short version: almost everything you write in Mentulis stays on your phone, and your conversations with the Companion are not stored anywhere at all.

Last updated: 5 September 2026

Where your information actually lives

This is the whole picture, in one table.

WhatWhere it is storedCan we see it?
Mood check-ins Your phone only No
Journal, gratitude, worry entries Your phone only No
Self-check results (GAD-7, PHQ-9) Your phone only No
Thought records Your phone only No
Companion conversations Nowhere — discarded after each reply No
Your email and password Firebase Authentication Email yes, password no
Profile — name, country, province Firebase Firestore Yes

In plain terms: we hold your account, not your contents. The things you would least want read — what you wrote at 2am, how you scored, what you have been worrying about — are on your device, and we have no copy of them.

Your conversations with the Companion

When you send a message, it goes to our server, which passes it to the AI model that generates the reply. The reply comes back to you and the exchange is discarded. It is not written to a database, not saved to your phone, and not kept in a log.

This means there is no chat history to browse — and no chat history to be breached, requested by a court, or sold in an acquisition. We think that trade is worth it.

Along with your message we send the conversational tone you chose in Personalize (warm, direct or quiet). Nothing else about you is attached.

Your account

Signing in uses Firebase Authentication, a Google service. It holds your email address and a securely hashed password. We can see your email address; nobody, including us, can see your password.

If you fill in your name, country of origin or province, those are stored in Firebase Firestore under your account. They are optional — the app works without them — and they are used only to personalise what the app shows you.

Optional backup to your own Google Drive

You can turn on backup, which copies your on-device wellness data to the hidden app-data area of your own Google Drive. That copy lives in your Google account, not ours. We can only ever see the single file the app created — never anything else in your Drive — and the backup never passes through Mentulis servers.

It is off by default. If you never turn it on, nothing is ever copied anywhere.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell or rent your data to anyone.
  • We do not share it with third parties for their own purposes.
  • We do not use your entries or conversations to train AI models.
  • We do not run ads, and the app contains no advertising ID and no analytics SDK.
  • We do not report anything to an employer, insurer or anyone else — including where an organisation provides Mentulis to you.

Who processes data for us

  • Google Firebase — authentication and profile storage
  • Railway — hosting for the server that relays Companion messages
  • Anthropic — the AI model that generates Companion replies

These providers process data on our instructions in order to run the service. They are not permitted to use it for their own purposes.

Security

All traffic between the app and our services is encrypted in transit using HTTPS/TLS. Data held in Firebase is encrypted at rest by Google.

No system is perfectly secure. The strongest protection here is structural rather than technical: most of your information is not in our custody in the first place.

Your choices and rights

  • Delete everything you have written, keeping your account — You → Preferences → Delete my data.
  • Delete your account and all data — see Delete your account. No waiting period, and you do not need to contact us first.
  • Export a summary as a PDF — Tools → Share with therapist.
  • Turn backup off at any time in Preferences.

Depending on where you live you may also have rights to access, correct or restrict use of your personal information, and to complain to a regulator. Write to hello@mentulis.com and we will help.

Children

Mentulis is not intended for anyone under 16. We do not knowingly collect information from children. If you believe a child has created an account, email us and we will remove it.

This website

mentulis.com sets no tracking cookies and runs no advertising or analytics scripts. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address as part of serving them. The breathing exercise and mood check-in on the homepage run entirely in your browser and send nothing anywhere.

Changes

If we change this policy in a way that materially affects you, we will tell you in the app or by email before it takes effect.

Mentulis is not a medical service or an emergency service.

Nobody monitors what you write, and no one is watching for signs that you are in danger. If you are in crisis, contact your local emergency number or a crisis line.

Contact

Questions about privacy, or to exercise any of the rights above: hello@mentulis.com
