Privacy policy

Last updated: October 1, 2026

Mentulis helps you look after your wellbeing, so we treat what you share with it as sensitive. This policy explains what we collect, why, where it goes and the choices you have. In short: most of what you put into Mentulis stays on your phone, encrypted, and we never sell your data or show ads.

1. Who we are

Mentulis is published by [Company legal name], [Registered address, Ontario, Canada] (“we”, “us”). We are responsible for your personal information under this policy. Questions or requests: info@mentulis.com.

2. What stays on your phone

Your answers during setup (such as age range, how you’ve been feeling, your goals), your mood check-ins, journal entries, tool and self-test results, games played, your Sprout’s progress and your chats with Pip are stored only on your device, in an encrypted database. We cannot see them.

Deleting the app, or using “Reset my data” in Profile, erases this information. “Export my data” gives you a copy at any time.

3. What we process, and why

We only collect what we need to provide a feature you choose to use:

  • Talking to Pip (AI companion): to write a reply, your most recent messages (up to 20) are sent over an encrypted connection to our server and to our AI provider, Anthropic. We do not store the content of your chats on our servers. We keep a daily message count linked to an anonymous ID to apply the free limit. Anthropic processes the messages only to produce the reply, does not use them to train its models, and may keep them for a short period (up to 30 days) for safety and abuse monitoring before deleting them.
  • Account and backup (optional, when available): if you create an account, we store your email address and an encrypted backup of your app data so you can restore it on a new phone.
  • Subscriptions: payments are handled by Google Play or the App Store. We receive confirmation of your subscription status, not your card details.
  • Psychologist sessions (adults in Ontario): when you book, we share your name, email, the chosen psychologist and session time with that psychologist, and payment is processed by Stripe. What you discuss in sessions is held by your psychologist, who is the custodian of that health information under Ontario’s Personal Health Information Protection Act (PHIPA).
  • Waitlist (this website): if you join, we store your email, language, phone type, your consent and the date, only to tell you about the launch. Every email has an unsubscribe link.
  • Support: if you email us, we use what you send to help you.

4. Health information and consent

Mood, feelings and anything you write about your mental health are sensitive. We ask for your explicit consent during setup before you use Mentulis, and you can withdraw it at any time by resetting your data or deleting the app. Where the law requires a legal basis (for example the GDPR in Europe and the UK), we rely on your explicit consent for health-related data and on our contract with you to provide the app.

5. What we never do

  • We never sell or rent your personal information.
  • We never show ads or share data with advertisers.
  • This website does not use tracking or advertising cookies.

6. Where your data is processed

Our servers run on Google Cloud (Firebase) in Montréal, Canada. Our AI provider, Anthropic, processes chat messages in the United States, and Stripe, Google and Apple may process payment information in other countries. These providers are bound by contracts that require them to protect your information. Where personal information leaves Canada, the European Economic Area or the UK, it is protected by appropriate safeguards such as standard contractual clauses.

7. How long we keep it

  • Chat content: not stored by us; daily message counts are deleted after 30 days.
  • Account and backup: until you delete your account.
  • Booking and payment records: as long as required by tax and professional rules (usually up to 7 years).
  • Waitlist: until launch plus 12 months, or until you unsubscribe.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live (including Canada’s PIPEDA, Quebec’s Law 25, the EU and UK GDPR and US state laws), you can ask to access, correct, delete or receive a copy of your personal information, withdraw consent, or object to or restrict processing. Much of this you can do yourself in the app (Export my data, Reset my data). For anything else, email info@mentulis.com and we will reply within 30 days. You can also complain to your privacy regulator, such as the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada, the Commission d’accès à l’information du Québec, or your local data protection authority.

9. Age

Mentulis is for people aged 16 and over. Psychologist sessions are for adults 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 16; if you think a child has used Mentulis, contact us and we will delete their information.

10. Security

Data on your phone is encrypted, with the key kept in your phone’s secure storage. You can add a PIN or fingerprint lock. Data in transit uses encryption (HTTPS/TLS), and access to our systems is limited to people who need it. No system is perfectly secure; if a breach affects you, we will tell you and the relevant regulators as the law requires.

11. Changes

If we change this policy in a meaningful way, we will tell you in the app before the change takes effect.

Mentulis is not an emergency service. If you’re in danger, call your local emergency number (911 in North America, 112 in Europe, 999 in the UK) or find a free, confidential helpline at findahelpline.com.
