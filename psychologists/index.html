When self-help isn’t enough, book a private video session with a psychologist registered in Ontario. One session or a package, no waiting lists, no referral needed.

How it works

  1. 1

    Choose

    Read each psychologist’s profile: approach, languages, what they help with.

  2. 2

    Book

    Pick one session or a package, then a time that suits you. Free changes up to 24 hours before.

  3. 3

    Meet by video

    Your psychologist emails a secure video link and a short intake form before your first session.

Who can book Adults 18 and over who will be in Ontario during their sessions; that’s where our psychologists are licensed to practise.

Privacy What you discuss stays between you and your psychologist, who keeps your records under Ontario’s health privacy law (PHIPA). Mentulis never sees session notes.

Insurance Sessions are usually covered by workplace or student plans that include psychology. You’ll get a receipt with the psychologist’s registration number.

Sessions are not for emergencies. If you are in crisis, call 911 or 988 (Suicide Crisis Helpline, Canada).

What we look for

  • Registered with the College of Psychologists and Behaviour Analysts of Ontario, in good standing
  • Professional liability insurance
  • A secure, PHIPA-compliant video platform and record keeping
  • Comfortable with evidence-based, short-term work (CBT, ACT and similar)
Get in touch
