1. Mentulis is not medical care

This is the most important thing on this page, so it goes first.

Mentulis is a self-help and wellbeing app. It is not a medical device, not a diagnosis, not treatment, and not a substitute for a doctor, therapist, psychologist or any other licensed professional. The Mentulis Companion is artificial intelligence. It is not a therapist, it is not supervised by one, and it can be wrong.

The self-check questionnaires in the app (GAD-7 and PHQ-9) are widely used screening tools. A score is not a diagnosis. Only a qualified clinician can diagnose a mental health condition.

Mentulis is not an emergency service.

It does not monitor what you write, and nobody is watching for signs that you are in danger. If you are in crisis or thinking about harming yourself, contact your local emergency number or a crisis line immediately. The app lists crisis resources and they are free to everyone — we never put them behind a subscription.

Never delay seeking professional help, or disregard advice you have been given, because of something you read in Mentulis.
