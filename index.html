Privacy

What we keep is deliberately boring.

Your mood check-ins, journal entries and self-check results are stored on your phone. Not on our servers. We cannot read them, because we do not have them.

Your account — your email, and a name and country if you choose to add them — is stored with Firebase so you can sign in. That is the part that leaves your phone, and it is the only part.

Conversations with the Companion are never stored, on your phone or ours. Each message is sent, answered and discarded. There is no chat history, which also means there is none to leak.
