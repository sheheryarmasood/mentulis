Questions, answered

Is Mentulis therapy?

No. Mentulis is a wellbeing app with self-help tools based on cognitive behavioural therapy (CBT) and related techniques. It doesn’t diagnose or treat any condition. If you need treatment, you can book a registered psychologist in the app (adults in Ontario) or talk to your doctor.

Who is it for?

Anyone aged 16 and over who wants small, everyday ways to handle stress, worry, low mood or sleep, especially students and busy working people. Psychologist sessions are for adults 18+ in Ontario.

What happens to my data?

Your journal, check-ins and chat history are stored on your phone, encrypted. When you talk to Pip, your recent messages are sent securely to generate a reply and aren’t stored on our servers. We never sell data or show ads. Read the full privacy policy for details.

What if I’m in crisis?

Mentulis is not an emergency service. The “Need help now” button is on every screen, free, and lists crisis lines for your country. If you’re in immediate danger, call your local emergency number.

When can I download it?

Mentulis is coming to Android first, then iPhone. Join the waitlist and we’ll email you on launch day.

Which languages?

English and French, everywhere in the app.
