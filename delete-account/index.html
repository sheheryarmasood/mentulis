Delete your account

Last updated: October 18, 2026

You can delete your Mentulis account and everything linked to it at any time, yourself, in the app. It takes effect straight away: there is no waiting period and you do not need to contact us.

  1. Delete your account in the app
  2. What is deleted
  3. What is kept, and for how long
  4. Can’t use the app?

1. Delete your account in the app

  • Open Mentulis and tap your Sprout at the top to open Profile.
  • Scroll to “Data & support” and tap “Delete my account”.
  • Confirm. If Google asks, choose your account again to prove it’s you.

2. What is deleted

  • Your account: email address, first name, country, gender, language and account ID.
  • Your progress backup: your Sprout, seeds, shop items, streaks and settings.
  • Everything stored on this phone: journal, moods, test results, tool entries and chats with Pip.

3. What is kept, and for how long

  • Daily Pip message counts and hashed network addresses: deleted automatically after 30 days.
  • Premium status (which plan and until when): deleted automatically 30 days after it ends.
  • Chat messages may be kept by our AI provider, Anthropic, for up to 30 days for safety monitoring, then deleted. We do not store them.
  • Deleting your account does not cancel a subscription. Cancel it in Google Play or the App Store, which keep their own payment records.

4. Can’t use the app?

If you no longer have the app or your phone, email info@mentulis.com from the email address of your Mentulis account and ask us to delete it. We will delete your account and backup within 30 days and confirm by email.

Only want to clear this phone and keep your account? Use “Reset my data” in Profile instead.

Mentulis is not an emergency service. If you’re in danger, call your local emergency number (911 in North America, 112 in Europe, 999 in the UK) or find a free, confidential helpline at findahelpline.com.
