How can we help?

If you are in crisis right now, please don’t wait for an email reply. Call your local emergency number or find a helpline at findahelpline.com.

How do I cancel Premium?

Open Google Play → your profile → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → Mentulis → Cancel. On iPhone: Settings → your name → Subscriptions. Uninstalling the app does not cancel a subscription.

How do I delete my data?

In the app, go to Profile → Reset my data. This erases everything stored on your phone. If you created an account, you can also ask us to delete it by email.

I forgot my app PIN

On the lock screen, tap “Forgot PIN”. For your privacy, this resets the app and erases your data on that phone, so export your data from time to time.

Reminders don’t arrive

Check that notifications are allowed for Mentulis in your phone’s settings, and that battery saver isn’t stopping the app in the background.

Booking or session questions

For changes to a session, contact your psychologist directly or use the booking in the app. For payment issues, email us.
